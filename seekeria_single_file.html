Origin & Portfolio Context

Why I Built SeekerIA

I wanted to explore what happens when a traditional job search is treated as an engineering problem.

Instead of manually opening multiple job boards every day, I built an autonomous workflow that collects information, filters noise, evaluates relevance and delivers the resulting signal through Telegram.

The project sits at the intersection of AI agents, automation, data engineering and practical software development.

Foundations

Engineering Principles

LOCAL-FIRST AI

Where practical, inference is performed on private infrastructure.

AUTOMATION FIRST

Repeated manual work should become a pipeline.

SIGNAL OVER VOLUME

More job listings do not necessarily mean better results.

MODULAR ARCHITECTURE

Sources, evaluation and delivery layers remain replaceable.

HUMAN IN THE LOOP

AI prioritizes and drafts; the final decision remains with the user.
